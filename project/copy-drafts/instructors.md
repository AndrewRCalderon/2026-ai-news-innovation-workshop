## Andrew Calderon

**Title/role**: Network Weaver, Journalism + Design
Lab + Adjunct Professor, CUNY Craig Newmark Graduate School of
Journalism.

**Bio**: Andrew Rodriguez Calderón is a computational journalist turned
product designer and relational technologist. He was a data project lead at The
Marshall Project, where his reporting on criminal justice earned a
Pulitzer Prize, with bylines also in The New York Times, Politico, and
Mother Jones. He is now a Network Weaver at the
Journalism + Design Lab, building community infrastructure to equip people in news deserts with journalism skills and training, and teaches Engagement Journalism at CUNY's Craig Newmark
Graduate School of Journalism. He holds a data journalism degree from
Columbia. To learn more about his work, check out his [website](https://andrewrcalderon.com/about.html).

## Adiel Kaplan

**Title/role**: Program Director, Tow-Knight Center

**Bio**: An investigative and data journalist, Adiel Kaplan leads the
design of the Tow-Knight Center's programs, prototypes, and research.
She writes about what the team is learning and builds the center's AI
lessons and tools. Before the center, she was a reporter and editor
with NBC News's Investigative Unit, covering climate, criminal justice,
healthcare, and labor. She teaches investigative reporting for Columbia
Journalism School's Toni Stabile Center and Data Program and leads the
Global Investigative Journalism Oral History Project with Incite
Institute.
