# Yesterday's Debrief

> Before we add anything new, let's look at what we covered yesterday.

## 1. What You Built

Yesterday ended with an hour of open project time. Some of you got further than others, and that's fine. We want to talk about what you observed, what happened while you were working, in the code and in yourself, before we move on.

### Self Reflection

- What did you try to build yesterday, in one sentence?
- What actually got built by the end of the day?
- If those two answers are different, why?
- How did you feel while working on your project?

### What worked

- What's one moment where Claude Desktop did something that genuinely helped?
- Was there a prompt or approach that worked better than you expected?

## 2. Where You Got Stuck

Getting stuck isn't a sign you did something wrong. It's information, and a lot of today is built to respond directly to the kinds of friction that showed up yesterday.

- Where did you get stuck, specifically? Not "AI didn't work," but a moment you got blocked.
- Did Claude misunderstand what you wanted, or did you not know what to ask for?
- Was there a point where you weren't sure if Claude was on the right track before you'd spent time finding out?

> **Exercise: Talk It Through**
>
> - Time: ~10 minutes.
> - Description: Take a minute to read back over the questions above on your own. Then let's have an exploratory conversation drawing from them. What comes up from your experience yesterday? Jump in when something resonates, build on what someone else says, or share your own moment. It doesn't need to go in order.
> - Deliverable: Nothing written. Come ready to name one specific moment, either something that worked or something you got stuck on, when we open it up.
