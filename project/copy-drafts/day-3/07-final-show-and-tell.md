# Final Show & Tell

> Three days ago this was a problem statement on a whiteboard. Now it's something you built.

## 1. Format

More formal than Day 2's show and tell, one more round. 5 minutes each, everyone presents, everyone gives feedback.

- Say what your problem statement or hypothesis is, why it matters.
- Show what you built, live if you can. Remember to highlight key learning from the class that informed your product design or ideation.
- Name some takeaways and reflections on AI use in news and product.

## 2. Closing

That's the workshop. Whatever you built this week is a starting point, not a finished product, the same way your problem statement on Day 1 was a starting point. What you actually leave with is the practice: prompting, planning, writing a spec, thinking about who's using what you build and what happens when it's wrong. That part travels with you past this week.

> Keep building. The best way to get better at this is the same way you got better this week: build something, show it to people, listen to what they say, build again.
