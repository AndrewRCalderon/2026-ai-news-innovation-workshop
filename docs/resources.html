AI Tools Landscape

Moved here from Day 1's AI Tools session, since a tool list goes stale fast and this page is easier to keep current than a scheduled lesson. Not exhaustive — a survey of what's worth knowing about, by category.

Text & Writing

  • Claude — Anthropic. Best for analysis, ideation, writing. Free (limited) / Pro $20/mo.
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI. General-purpose, brainstorming. Free (limited) / Plus $20/mo / Pro $200/mo.
  • Gemini — Google. Research and writing, especially with Gmail/Docs. Free / Advanced (paid).
  • Perplexity — AI search with real-time web access. Free (limited) / Pro $20/mo.

Coding & Development

  • Claude Code — Reads your codebase, writes code, runs commands. Included with Claude Pro. This is what Day 2 introduces.
  • GitHub Copilot — Inline autocomplete as you type. Free (limited) / Pro $10/mo.
  • Cursor — VS Code with AI built in — tab completion, chat. Free / Pro $20/mo.
  • Replit — Write, run, and deploy in the browser — no local setup. Free / Boost $13/mo.

Data Analysis & Research

  • Claude (file upload) — Upload documents or datasets, ask questions about them. Included with Pro.
  • Jupyter + Claude — Interactive Python for data journalism and research. Free.
  • Airtable — Database with AI-assisted organization. Free / Pro $12/mo.

Images & Visuals

  • DALL-E — OpenAI. Text-to-image for illustrations, mockups. Pay per image, $0.02–$0.08.
  • Midjourney — Art-focused image generation. $10–120/mo.
  • Canva + Magic Design — Templates plus AI-assisted layout. Free / Pro $13/mo.

Audio & Video

  • ElevenLabs — Natural-sounding text-to-speech. Free (limited) / Starter $11/mo.
  • Descript — Edit video by editing the transcript; podcast/video production. Free (limited) / Pro $24/mo.
  • Synthesia — AI-avatar video from text — explainers, training. $30–60/mo.

Research & Learning

  • Perplexity — Answers with cited sources — research, fact-checking.
  • Zotero + AI plugins — Organize and summarize research papers. Free, open-source.
  • Google Scholar — Peer-reviewed paper search. Free.

Automation & Workflows

  • Make — Connect apps, automate repetitive tasks. Free (limited) / Pro $15/mo.
  • Zapier — No-code workflows between services. Free (limited) / Pro $19/mo.
