Where Can You Take This?

Not "AI is changing journalism." The specific thing you just learned: building, not just prompting.

2:00 PM·45 MIN·Lecture
01

A Culture Coming Back

Newsroom public code repos fell 80% from 2016 to 2024. NYT posted zero in 2024.

By early 2026, about 37% of new newsroom repos showed AI-assisted building signals, growth that accelerated sharply since September 2025. Not being a programmer is less of a wall than it used to be.

02

Named Roles, Right Now

Newsroom Architect, Politico
Bridges editorial and technical strategy.
Computational Journalist, ProPublica
Built OCR and transcription tools before joining.
Senior Editor, AI Innovation, CNN
Prototypes and deploys tools with reporters.

"Coding and product development" ranks 3rd most important AI use case for publishers, 81% importance, in a 50+ country survey.

03

You Don't Need to Be Hired to Do This

The clearest examples are individual reporters, not engineering teams.

  • A Reuters correspondent with no coding background has built 14 tools.
  • One journalist solo-built and maintains 50+ Claude Code Skills for journalism.
  • A researcher's tipsheet generator, tested against 7 Pulitzer investigations, surfaced useful leads about half the time.
04

A Realistic Read

A controlled trial found experienced developers using AI tools were 19% slower, while believing they were 20% faster.

Not a straight line to faster at everything. Most useful exactly where you have no other option: no budget, no engineer, no time to learn to code the traditional way. Northwestern already runs an investigative AI challenge built on this exact combination, open to freelancers and students.
