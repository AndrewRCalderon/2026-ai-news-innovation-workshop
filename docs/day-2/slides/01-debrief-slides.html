Yesterday's Debrief

Before we add anything new, let's look at what actually happened yesterday.

10:00 AM·20 MIN·Discussion
01

What You Built

The point of a debrief isn't to compare. It's to observe what happened while you were working, in the code and in yourself.

  • What did you try to build yesterday, in one sentence?
  • What actually got built by the end of the day?
  • If those two answers are different, why?

What worked

  • One moment where Claude Desktop genuinely helped?
  • A prompt or approach that worked better than expected?
02

Where You Got Stuck

Getting stuck isn't a sign you did something wrong. It's information, and today is built to respond to it directly.

  • Where did you get stuck, specifically?
  • Did Claude misunderstand you, or did you not know what to ask for?
  • Were you unsure Claude was on the right track before you'd spent time finding out?

Talk It Through

Read the questions above, then open it up as a group. Jump in, build on each other, share your own moment. No particular order.
