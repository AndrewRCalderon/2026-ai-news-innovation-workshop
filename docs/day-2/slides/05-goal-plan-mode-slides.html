Your Goal & Plan Mode

Before Claude touches any code, it can research and propose an approach first.

12:30 PM·30 MIN·Hands-on
01

What Plan Mode Is

Plan mode tells Claude to research and propose changes without making them. Edits stay blocked until you approve the plan.

Enter it with Shift+Tab, or prefix a prompt with /plan.

02

When to Use It

Letting Claude jump straight to coding can produce code that solves the wrong problem.

If you could describe the diff in one sentence, skip the plan.

A project goal you're still figuring out is exactly the kind of uncertainty plan mode is for.

03

State Your Goal

Not building yet, that's after lunch. Just a clear-eyed look at your project before committing to an approach.
