Show & Tell + Critique

Two days of building, out loud. The goal is honest feedback you can use tomorrow.

3:30 PM·90 MIN·Discussion
01

Format

Everyone presents, and everyone gives feedback. A few minutes each, structured the same way.

  • Show what you built, live if you can. Broken is fine.
  • What you set out to build, and where it landed.
  • One thing you'd try next.
02

Giving a Useful Critique

"I like it" isn't feedback.

Ask this

  • What problem, for whom?
  • Riskiest assumption?
  • What would build trust?

Not this

  • "That's cool," unexplained
  • Rebuilding their idea into yours
  • Polish feedback on an unproven idea

Structured critique

Format: For each presenter: what's working, the likely next problem, one honest question.
