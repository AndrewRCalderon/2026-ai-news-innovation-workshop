01

What Plan Mode Is

Plan mode tells Claude to research and propose changes without making them. It reads files, runs commands to explore, and writes a plan, but edits stay blocked until you approve it.*

Enter it with Shift+Tab, or by prefixing a prompt with /plan.* Once the plan is ready, you choose: approve and let Claude build, approve but review edits one at a time, or send it back to keep planning.

02

When to Use It

Letting Claude jump straight to coding can produce code that solves the wrong problem.* That's the whole case for it. But planning has real overhead, and not every task needs it.

If you could describe the diff in one sentence, skip the plan.*

Fixing a typo or renaming a variable doesn't need a plan. A project goal you're still figuring out, on the other hand, is exactly the kind of uncertainty plan mode is for.

03

State Your Goal

This isn't about building yet, that happens after lunch. It's about getting a clear-eyed look at your project from Claude before you commit to an approach.

🎯 Exercise: Plan Before You Build

Time: ~15 minutes.

Description: Open plan mode on your Day 1 project. State your goal for the rest of the workshop in plain language, then let Claude research and propose an approach. Don't approve it yet, just read it.

Deliverable: A plan you can bring back after lunch, whether or not you end up using it as-is.
