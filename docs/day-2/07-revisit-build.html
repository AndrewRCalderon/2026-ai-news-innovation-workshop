01

Bring Back Your Goal

This isn't starting over. It's the same project, with better tools than you had this morning.

Pull up the plan mode output from earlier and the spec checklist from Spec-Driven Development. Before you touch any code, take a few minutes to actually look at what changed:

  • Does your CLAUDE.md still describe what you're actually building, or does it need an update?
  • Looking at your plan from this morning, does it still hold up, or did something you learned today change it?
  • If you ran your project through the spec checklist right now, what's missing?
02

Build

Most of this session is just time to work. The instructor will circulate one-on-one rather than teaching from the front, so use that time.

🛠️ Build Time

Time: The rest of this session.

Description: Apply today's tools to your Day 1 project. Update your CLAUDE.md if it needs it, write or revise a spec for what you're building next, then build.

Deliverable: Visible progress on your project, not a finished product. Something to show and talk about next.

If you're not sure where to start

  • Pick one small piece rather than redoing everything from scratch.
  • Apply the spec checklist to just your next feature, not the whole project.
  • Ask Claude directly: "given everything we've covered today, how would you approach this differently than yesterday?"
