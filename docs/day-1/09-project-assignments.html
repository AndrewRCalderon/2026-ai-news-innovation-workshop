01

What You Should Have By Now

  • A one-sentence problem, a hypothesis ("If I use AI to [specific task], then [specific outcome] will happen for [specific person/group]"), and a goal, from Problem Statement.
  • A sense of how a coding design loop works: describe, plan, build, review, test, iterate.
  • A forked repo with a day-1 branch, your docs/submissions/your-name/ folder, and CLAUDE.md and SUBMISSION.md copied into it, from Fork & Submit.
  • A workshop buddy or two alongside you.

If any of those is missing, tell the instructors.

02

Workshop Activity

🎯 Exercise: Try It Out

Time: 45 minutes, with your workshop buddy.

Description: Open your project folder in Claude Desktop's Code tab. Your CLAUDE.md should guide you through some questions to calibrate your coding experience. Read the file as well. If you don't understand something, ask Claude. Once you are situated, give Claude your problem statement and hypothesis in Plan mode, and see what it proposes before it touches anything. Work alongside your buddy: check in on each other's progress, compare what you're each seeing, and ask each other questions before you ask an instructor.

Deliverable: Real, early progress, committed and pushed through the fork/PR workflow from Fork & Submit.

03

Tips for This Hour

  • Start in Plan mode, remember to break things up into discrete tasks.
  • Read the diff before you accept it. This is an important habit, and the easiest one to skip when you're moving fast.
  • Stuck? Ask your buddy first, then Claude, then the instructor.
  • Notice friction, flow, and gaps in knowledge.
04

What Happens Next

Whatever state your project is in at the end of this hour is exactly where Day 2 picks up. Tomorrow is about iteration and depth: prompting well, setting up CLAUDE.md properly, using Skills, planning before you build, and writing a spec instead of prompting straight to code, all applied to the project you started today.
