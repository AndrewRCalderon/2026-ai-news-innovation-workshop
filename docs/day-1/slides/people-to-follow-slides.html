People to Follow

The AI + journalism conversation moves faster than any single article — so follow the people, not just the headlines.

Resource — not a scheduled session
01

Drawing Inspiration From Others

AI moves fast — what worked last month might be outdated next month. The best way to stay informed is following people actively working on AI, journalism, and the intersection of the two.

  • They document what they're building and why.
  • They ask hard questions about AI's impact.
  • They share concrete examples from their own reporting.
  • They're thinking ahead about what's coming next.
